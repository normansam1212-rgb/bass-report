Bank Fishing

accessibility ranked
How this works: Each water is scored 1–10 on how good it is for bank fishing — no boat needed. Factors: public shore access, parking & facilities, shoreline length, water depth from bank, structure & cover, crowd level, and whether the fishery is wade-friendly. A–B rated = pull up and fish. C = workable with some effort. D = difficult from shore.

Bass Activity

live fishing conditions across Texas
How this works: All 109 Texas lakes are scored using the same real-time forecast model as the Report tab — weather, barometric trend, water temperature, wind, moon phase, and each lake's intrinsic characteristics. Live Score = where to fish right now. Fishery Quality = intrinsic reputation (trophy waters, structure, clarity). Observed = citizen-science bass sightings from iNaturalist, which skews toward urban lakes with more nature photographers.
